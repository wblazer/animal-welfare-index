# Manual source review queue

Batch 1 was reviewed on 2026-09-01 and the decisions below were applied to the catalog.

## Preferences established

- Favor first-order reasoning about animals over movement operations, careers, media monitoring, and field-building.
- A single excellent article can be an entry. Do not preserve an empty or irrelevant root around it.
- Informal writing can be valuable when it develops a substantive argument; institutional prestige is not required.
- Apply quality over quantity even to directly relevant animal-welfare writing. Short, inconclusive, or predominantly meta archives can be omitted.
- Close alignment with the project’s AI motivation does not compensate for writing that is mainly about building or coordinating the field.
- The source itself should substantially develop ethical reasoning about animals. Rigorous strategy, technical alternatives, public messaging research, or documentation alone is not enough.
- Predominantly visual sources are outside the core reading list even when their documentation is excellent.

## Batch 1 decisions

| Source | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [A quick economic model of humane meat consumption](https://tejassubramaniam.github.io/blog/free-range/) | Keep the article; remove the empty Substack root | The article is excellent first-order economic reasoning. The nominal publication contains no posts. |
| [On the Vedge](https://rorycardy.substack.com/) | Keep | [Is a Meat Tax the Wrong Tool for the Right Goal?](https://rorycardy.substack.com/p/is-a-meat-tax-the-wrong-tool-for) is good substantive policy analysis. |
| [CAFT](https://abolishfur.substack.com/) | Remove | Too focused on movement-level strategy. |
| [Special Interests](https://ulazarosa.substack.com/) | Remove | The relevant ethical argument is too broad and animal-specific material is sparse. |
| [Animal Advocacy Careers](https://www.animaladvocacycareers.org/) | Remove | Career and talent material is too meta. |
| [DawnWatch](https://dawnwatch.substack.com/) | Remove | Media monitoring favors quantity and news coverage over durable, high-quality reasoning. |
| [Better Life, Better World](https://mattball.substack.com/) | Keep | This is the desired kind of substantive, pragmatic reasoning about helping animals. |
| [duck dive](https://duckdives.substack.com/) | Remove | The archive is small, less conclusive, and somewhat meta; quality matters more than adding another relevant publication. |
| [Moral Circulation](https://ourexpandingcircle.substack.com/) | Keep | The focused, first-order animal-ethics writing is an excellent fit. |
| [Shot on Goal](https://frontieranimals.substack.com/) | Remove | Despite motivating this project, the publication is too focused on the AI-and-animals field itself rather than first-order reasoning. |
