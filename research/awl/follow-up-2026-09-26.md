## Research notes

- **The Importance of Wild-Animal Suffering:** the Brian Tomasik essay is hosted at Center on Long-Term Risk (`https://longtermrisk.org/research/the-importance-of-wild-animal-suffering/`). The matching Reducing Suffering URL returns 404. The CLR page is public HTML with about 8,900 words.
- **Conservation vs. Wild-Animal Suffering:** Europe PMC returns 403 to scripted requests and does not host full text. Its metadata points to a bioRxiv PDF; bioRxiv rate-limited the orb.
- **PhilPapers records:** both returned Cloudflare verification pages in scripted and browser requests.
- **WBI Studies Repository:** Animal Sentience article pages expose about 700 words of HTML metadata and abstract text. The journal policy says articles default to CC BY, with CC BY-NC available on request. The checked PDF returned 403 to a scripted request.
